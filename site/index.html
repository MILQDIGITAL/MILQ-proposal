    ProcessFour steps, in order

    Clear first. Then fast.

    We do not start designing until we can explain your numbers back to you. It is the quickest way to a store that works.

    01DiagnoseWhere is the store actually leaking?
    [ Weeks 1–3 ]
    • Profitability & P&L audit
    • Analytics and tracking review
    • Customer and competitor research
    • Tech stack and app audit

    We dig through the numbers, the stack and the customer journey until we can say plainly what is holding growth back. You get a written diagnosis and a prioritised plan, whether you build with us or not.

    02DesignMake the right thing obvious.
    [ Weeks 3–6 ]
    • Information architecture
    • UX flows and prototypes
    • UI and design system
    • Content and merchandising plan

    Mobile-first prototypes tested against the problems we found, not against a mood board. Every screen has a job and a metric attached.

    03BuildShopify, done properly.
    [ Weeks 6–11 ]
    • Custom Shopify / Plus themes
    • Integrations: ERP, 3PL, POS
    • Data migration and redirects
    • Performance and accessibility QA

    Clean, documented theme code your team can run without us, with the integrations wired in and every redirect mapped before launch day.

    04GrowLaunch is the starting line.
    [ Ongoing ]
    • CRO and A/B testing
    • SEO and AI visibility
    • Retention and Klaviyo
    • Monthly trading reviews

    A retainer built around outcomes: a monthly review, a test backlog and a team that already knows your store inside out.
